THE STONE HAS CRACKED

You are falling.

Not through space. Through what was solid. The stone that sealed the threshold has cracked — and you are the thing that slipped through.

Above you, the light of the world narrows to a pinprick. Below, something vast and patient breathes in the dark.

A golden thread glows at the edge of your vision. You did not bring it. It brought you.

THE GUIDE APPEARS
☿ HERMES
I · The Magician
Messages · Thresholds · Guidance
"You spoke truth to the twins. That's rarer than you'd think. I am Hermes — guide of souls, living and dead. The path ahead has five rivers. Each one takes something from you. You don't get to keep everything. Ready? No one ever is. Walk anyway."
💧 STYX · The River of Oaths
Cold and black as ink. The Styx binds unbreakable oaths. Even the gods swear by it. To cross, leave behind a promise you made that you know you cannot keep.
⚡ ZEUS — IV · The Emperor
"The Styx binds even me. But you are not a god — you are not yet bound. The sky above still holds your name. The oath you leave here will be remembered. Are you certain you want to continue?"
🌫️ LETHE · The River of Forgetting
Soft and gray as old milk. Shades drink here before returning to the world above. Release a memory you keep replaying — one that hurts, or holds you back.
☀️ APOLLO — XIX · The Sun
"Forgetting is a theft from yourself. I illuminate — I do not erase. The memory you surrender to Lethe will be lost to even my light. Are you certain?"
😞 ACHERON · The River of Pain
Deep and slow. Charon poles his boat on these waters. Admit a pain you carry that you did not deserve. Not guilt. Not regret. Undeserved wounding.
🌙 ARTEMIS — VII · The Chariot
"Pain is the quarry you have been tracking. You have caught it. Now — do you release it into the river, or let it consume you? The hunt does not end here unless you choose to stop."
🔥 PHLEGETHON · The River of Fire
It burns. It does not consume — it illuminates. Name an anger you still hold. Not the petty kind. The deep kind. The kind that shaped you.
🦉 ATHENA — VIII · Justice
"Anger is a weapon. Weapons require discipline. The fire river burns — but it also illuminates. Do not burn what you came here to save. Will wisdom hold the reins, or will the fire drive?"
😢 COCYTUS · The River of Lamentation
Fed by the tears of the damned and the grief of the living. Release a grief you have never fully expressed. Something you lost and never properly mourned.
🌾 DEMETER — III · The Empress
"I know grief. I made the world starve when Persephone was taken. The river of lamentation feeds on tears — but the surface still has fields that need tending. Come home. The harvest is not yet done."
