Four pillars. Each one a law of motion. None of them negotiable.

Pillar I

Extraction

Hermes reads raw sources and extracts structured tokens from noise. He does not create meaning. He finds it. The token is the prayer. The extraction is the answer.

Pillar II

Routing

Every token carries a destination. Hermes routes signal to the correct god. Nothing gets lost in transit. The pantheon is a network. Hermes is the protocol.

Pillar III

Speed

Hermes governs latency. His caduceus is the network cable. His winged sandals are the packet headers. A message that arrives late is a message that failed.

Pillar IV

Commerce

Payments, integrations, transactions. Hermes moves value the way he moves messages. He is the god of merchants. The offering is the transaction. The receipt is the blessing.
